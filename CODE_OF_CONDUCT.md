# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We want participation in the ASO-Light community to be a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background or identity.

## Our Standards

We expect community members to:

- Be kind, respectful, and considerate in all interactions
- Welcome differing viewpoints and give constructive feedback gracefully
- Focus on what is best for the community and the project
- Show empathy toward other members

Behaviour that makes others feel unwelcome or unsafe is not acceptable. This includes personal attacks, demeaning comments, unwelcome attention, publishing others' private information without consent, and any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Our Standards in Full

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1. Please read the full text for the complete list of expectations and enforcement guidelines.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces — the repository, issues, pull requests, and discussions — and when an individual is representing the project in public spaces.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, please report it to the maintainers via the email address listed on the GitHub organization profile, or through GitHub's reporting tools. All reports will be reviewed and handled with discretion.
